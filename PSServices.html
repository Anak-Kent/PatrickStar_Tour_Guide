Discover Boracay

Boracay is a famous Philippine island known for its white sand beaches, clear waters, beautiful sunsets, and exciting island activities.

Places to Explore

  • White Beach: Enjoy the sand, swimming, and spectacular sunsets.
  • D'Mall: Explore shops, restaurants, and local souvenirs.
  • Bulabog Beach: Discover water sports and a different coastal view.

Travel tip: Bring sunscreen, comfortable clothing, and beach essentials.

Discover Palawan

Palawan is celebrated for its limestone cliffs, turquoise lagoons, hidden beaches, and rich marine life. It is an excellent destination for nature lovers and island explorers.

Places to Explore

  • El Nido: Explore lagoons, limestone formations, and island-hopping routes.
  • Coron: Discover scenic lakes, snorkeling spots, and clear waters.
  • Puerto Princesa: Visit the city and explore the famous underground river area.

Travel tip: Book popular boat tours in advance and bring waterproof bags for your items.

Discover Bohol

Bohol is known for its unusual Chocolate Hills, tiny tarsiers, countryside scenery, and beautiful beaches. It offers a mix of nature, culture, and family-friendly sightseeing.

Places to Explore

  • Chocolate Hills: See the island's famous cone-shaped hills.
  • Tarsier Conservation Areas: Learn about the small native primates.
  • Panglao Island: Enjoy beaches, snorkeling, and coastal views.

Travel tip: Plan time for the countryside attractions and wear comfortable shoes.

Discover Siargao

Siargao is famous for surfing, palm-lined roads, natural pools, and relaxed island life. Visitors can enjoy both outdoor adventures and peaceful coastal scenery.

Places to Explore

  • Cloud 9: Visit the island's well-known surfing area.
  • Magpupungko Rock Pools: See natural tidal pools when conditions allow.
  • Sugba Lagoon: Enjoy the beautiful lagoon and its surrounding scenery.

Travel tip: Check tide and weather conditions before visiting natural pools or joining water activities.
